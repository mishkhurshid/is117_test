# The White T-Shirt Guide to Persuasion, Branding, and Design

This mini textbook explores how ordinary products become meaningful through persuasion, brand identity, and visual language. It follows one recurring example: a plain white T-shirt that can be framed in dramatically different ways without changing the shirt itself.

The guide is meant for first-year students who want a practical introduction to how meaning is built, communicated, and evaluated.

## How to Use This Guide

Read the chapters in order for the full progression from persuasion to archetype to visual language to a combined case study. Then use the final chapter as a bridge between design thinking and AI-assisted work.

Each chapter is designed to help you ask better questions:

- What does this product or message want the audience to feel or do?
- What identity is it expressing?
- How does its design language support or undermine that meaning?

The goal is not to memorize a formula. It is to become more aware of how meaning is built in the world around you.
